# Experimental software — use at your own risk

OpenFUSE is an independent preservation project developed with substantial help
from AI coding agents (“vibe-coded”). It is not an official Fender product, an
independently audited implementation, or a certified replacement for FUSE.

Automated tests, simulated devices and comparisons against original code establish
specific software properties. They do not establish correctness on a physical
amplifier, flash persistence, audible equivalence, recovery from a failed write,
or safety for every device and firmware version. No amplifier model is currently
claimed as hardware-validated by this project.

Bugs may cause loss or corruption of presets and backups, unexpected device
settings, interrupted audio, or device malfunction. Keep independent backups,
work on copies, and start with offline editing. Device connection and writes are
explicit actions. An error, timeout or cancellation after writing begins does
not imply the device was restored to its previous state.

Use this software only if you understand and accept these risks. The software is
provided AS IS, without warranty, subject to the terms of LICENSE and applicable
law. This statement does not override rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.
It is not a safety certification or a guarantee that no harm can occur.
